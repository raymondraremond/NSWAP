How Listings Work

Post What You Have.
State What You Need.

Every NSWAP listing is a simple two-part statement. You state exactly what item and size you currently have, and the size you actually need. This makes matching fast, clear, and accurate.

Listings are only visible to corps members in the same deployment state, keeping results relevant and local.
